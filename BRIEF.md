# Company Research & Justification Brief

## Company Chosen

**Company Name:** Home Constructors  
**Industry:** Construction and Building Services  
**Location:** Soshanguve, South Africa  
**Current Website:** Google Sites website

## How I Found The Company

I found Home Constructors through Google Maps while looking for local construction companies in Soshanguve. After viewing their online presence, I noticed that their website could be improved to better showcase their services and create a more professional image.

## Why I Chose This Company

I chose Home Constructors because it is a local construction company that provides important building and renovation services to the community. I wanted to redesign their website because I saw an opportunity to create a modern platform that better represents their work, services, and professionalism.

The company already had valuable services to offer, but their existing website did not fully highlight their capabilities or create a strong online presence.

## Why Their Website Needed A Redesign

After reviewing their existing Google Sites website, I identified a few areas that needed improvement:

- The overall design looked outdated and needed a more modern and professional appearance.
- The website did not have a strong visual impact to attract potential customers.
- The layout could be improved to make information easier for visitors to find.
- The services were not presented in a clear and engaging way.
- The website needed a more responsive design to work better on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop screens.
- The branding and user experience could be improved to better match a professional construction company.

## Redesign Solution

I redesigned the Home Constructors website using React, JavaScript, Tailwind CSS, and React Router. The new website focuses on creating a modern, responsive, and user-friendly experience.

The redesign includes a cleaner navigation system, a professional homepage, improved service sections, better image presentation, and a responsive layout that works across different screen sizes.

The goal of this redesign was to help Home Constructors have a stronger online presence and make it easier for potential customers to learn about their services and contact them.
